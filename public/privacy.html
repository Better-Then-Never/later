Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short

  • We collect what Later needs to work: your account, your profile, the capsules and messages you create, and the people you connect with.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Your messages and capsules are stored on Google Firebase servers. They are encrypted in transit and at rest, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. This removes your data from our live systems.

1. Who we are

Later is a mobile app for leaving photo "capsules" on a map and chatting with friends. It is operated by Better Than Never ("we", "us"). You can reach us at betterthannever.business@gmail.com. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

Account

  • Your email address and a password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it), or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account's email address and name.
  • Your name and username.

Profile

  • A profile picture and a background picture, if you add them.

Content you create

  • Capsules: the photo, title, note, colour, who may see it (only you, friends or everyone), the date it opens, and the location where you created it.
  • Messages: the text you send, capsules you share in a chat, when they were sent, and when the other person last read the chat (used for "Read" receipts and unread counts).
  • Favourites and pinned friends.

Your connections

  • Your friends, friend requests, the people you follow and who follow you, and the people you blocked.
  • In-app notifications (for example "Alice started following you").

Device and settings

  • A push notification token for each device you use, and whether it is Android or iOS.
  • Your notification settings, the chats you muted, and your time zone offset (so quiet hours follow your local time).
  • Some preferences (map look, starting camera, pinned friends) stay only on your device.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show you on the map, find capsules near you and pin a new capsule where you are. We store a location only as part of a capsule you create. We do not track or store your location history.

Camera and photos

With your permission, the app uses the camera to take capsule and profile photos and to scan friends' QR codes, and your photo library to pick a picture. Only the photos you choose to use are uploaded.

What we do not collect

  • The app may ask for access to your contacts and microphone. It does not read your contacts or record audio, and nothing from them is uploaded.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers.

3. How we use it, and why we may

  • To provide Later: create your account, show your profile, store and show capsules and messages to the people you chose, and deliver notifications. This is necessary to perform our agreement with you (the Terms of Use).
  • To keep Later safe: enforce blocks, prevent abuse, investigate reports and keep the service running. This is our legitimate interest in a secure service.
  • Device permissions (location, camera, photos, notifications) are used only after you allow them, and you can withdraw them at any time in your phone's settings.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who can see what

  • Everyone using Later can find you by username and see your name, username and pictures.
  • Anyone with your profile link or QR code can see your name and profile picture on our web page, even without the app.
  • Capsules are visible according to the setting you choose: only you, your friends, or everyone. Public capsules show their location on everyone's map.
  • Messages are visible to you and the friend you are talking to. "Clear history" deletes the conversation's messages for both of you.
  • People you block cannot message you or send you friend requests.

5. Service providers

We use these providers to run Later. They process data on our behalf and under their own security and data protection commitments:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging): stores your account, content and connections, and delivers notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform: shows the map.
  • Your phone's built-in location services (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone): turn a capsule's coordinates into a place name.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Apple Push Notification service on iPhones, through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We share personal data with others only if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or with your consent.

6. Where your data is stored

Our database (accounts, profiles, capsules, messages and connections) is located in the European Union. Photos and pictures are stored in the United States, and some processing by our providers (for example sending notifications and running server functions) also takes place there. For data that leaves the European Economic Area, Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • You can delete individual capsules, clear chats, unfriend, unfollow and change your pictures at any time.
  • When you delete your account (Settings, Delete account), we remove your profile, pictures, capsules, chats (for both participants), friend and follower links, settings and notification tokens from our live systems. Copies in backups are deleted when those backups are removed.
  • Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Much of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, write to betterthannever.business@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our providers, and access is limited by security rules so that people see only what is shared with them. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: do not use Later for information that must stay secret. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Children

Later is not for children under 13. In the European Economic Area and the UK you must be at least 16, or have permission from a parent or guardian where local law allows a lower age. If we learn that a child has an account without the required permission, we delete it. Parents can contact us at the address above.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as Later changes. We will change the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
